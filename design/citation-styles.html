How these were chosen. The current pattern works but treats every citation the same — same chip, same blockquote, no differentiation between a primary filing fact and a derived aggregator number. Each proposal above attacks a different axis of that problem:
  • 01 Provenance pills — minimum-diff upgrade to the existing chip strip.
  • 02 Numbered footnotes — cleanest reading, citations exit the prose entirely.
  • 03 Evidence cards — citations become the content; best in the Findings panel.
  • 04 Provenance bar — section-level lineage; best for macro & multi-source synthesis.
  • 05 Margin annotations — editorial & longform; cleanest for the deep-dive narrative tab.
  • 06 Inline receipts — densest; best for tables and KPI strips, also where it shines on a print view.
Most likely production direction: 01 for inline citation lists (lowest migration cost, replaces the current CitationList), plus 03 for evidence rows in the Findings panel (replaces CitationBlock where the quote & value matter most). Options 02, 04, and 05 are stretch ideas for the long-form pages like Pipeline reports and the Workspace verdict view.
