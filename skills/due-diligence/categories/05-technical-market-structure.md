## Purpose

Determine what the market is telling us about this stock's near-term direction, institutional participation, and structural price behavior. Technical and market structure analysis does not replace fundamental research — it complements it by revealing when the market is confirming or contradicting the thesis, and by identifying optimal entry and exit zones.

The core question: "What is the market telling us about this stock's near-term direction and institutional interest?"

Fundamental research tells you what to buy. Technical analysis tells you when to buy it and where the market has established meaningful price levels. Used together, they reduce the cost of being early and improve position management precision.

## Synthesis

After running all sub-skills, answer these integrative questions:
1. Is the **trend** clearly established (strong up/downtrend) or ambiguous (range-bound, conflicting signals)?
2. Is **volume** confirming the trend (accumulation on up days, distribution on down days) — or diverging?
3. Do **support levels** offer a logical entry zone with a well-defined risk/reward setup?
4. Is **options flow** consistent with the directional thesis — or are large players positioning against it?
5. Does the technical picture **align or conflict** with the fundamental thesis? If conflicting, understand why before acting.

## Scoring

**TM Score (0-100)** = Trend (35%) + Volume (25%) + Support/resistance (20%) + Options flow (20%)

- Trend score: derived from trend-momentum sub-skill; strong uptrend = 80-100, weak uptrend = 60-79, range-bound = 40-59, weak downtrend = 20-39, strong downtrend = 0-19
- Volume score: accumulation pattern (up-day volume > down-day volume, OBV rising with price) = 70-100; neutral = 40-69; distribution pattern (down-day volume dominates, OBV diverging) = 0-39
- Support/resistance score: stock positioned near strong support with clean upside to next resistance = 70-100; midpoint between levels = 40-69; at resistance with no support nearby = 0-39
- Options flow score: net bullish flow (put/call ratio <0.7, unusual call buying) = 70-100; neutral = 40-69; net bearish flow (put/call ratio >1.0, put buying dominating) = 0-39

Note: TM Score is a short-term signal. A low score does not invalidate a strong fundamental thesis — it signals to wait for a better entry. A high score alone is not sufficient to establish a position without fundamental support.

## Red Flags

- Death cross (50 DMA below 200 DMA) with accelerating downside momentum
- Volume expanding on down days and contracting on up days for 4+ consecutive weeks (classic distribution pattern)
- OBV diverging negatively from price (price making new highs but OBV declining — smart money selling into the rally)
- Put/call ratio >1.5 with large OTM put blocks — institutional hedging at scale
- Stock sitting directly at major resistance with no clear catalyst to break through
- RSI making lower highs while price makes higher highs (bearish momentum divergence)
- Relative volume 3x+ the 20-day average on a sharp down day with no news — potential institutional exit
- IV rank >80th percentile — options are expensive, expected move is large, risk of both directions is elevated

## Output

- TM Score (0-100) with sub-factor breakdown (Trend, Volume, Support/resistance, Options flow)
- Trend classification: strong uptrend / weak uptrend / range-bound / weak downtrend / strong downtrend
- Weinstein stage: Stage 1 (accumulation) / Stage 2 (markup) / Stage 3 (distribution) / Stage 4 (markdown)
- Support and resistance level table (3 levels each, with confidence rating)
- Volume pattern assessment: accumulation / neutral / distribution
- Options positioning summary: put/call ratio, unusual activity flags, max pain level, IV rank
- Entry zone recommendation for position management phase (best support level + invalidation level)
- Technical/fundamental alignment assessment: CONFIRMING / NEUTRAL / CONFLICTING
