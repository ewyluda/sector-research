## Purpose

Sentiment and narrative are the market's psychological layer — they determine how the same fundamental data gets interpreted, amplified, or ignored. A company with improving fundamentals can still underperform if sentiment is deteriorating and the narrative is broken. Conversely, a company with mediocre fundamentals can sustain an elevated multiple as long as the prevailing narrative holds. Understanding the current sentiment posture and dominant narrative helps identify entry/exit timing, multiple expansion or compression risk, and early inflection signals before they appear in price.

## Key Dimensions

| Dimension | Sub-Skill | Core Question |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| News Sentiment | news-sentiment | What is the volume, tone, and trajectory of news coverage over the past 30 days? |
| Social Signals | social-signals | What is the retail and social media crowd doing — and how extreme is their positioning? |
| Market Narrative | market-narrative | What story is the market telling about this stock, and is that story changing? |
| Institutional Positioning | institutional-positioning | What are large institutions buying or selling, and what does analyst sentiment reveal? |

## Synthesis

After running all sub-skills, answer these integrative questions:
1. Is news sentiment **improving, stable, or deteriorating** over the trailing 30 days, and is momentum accelerating?
2. Are social signals **confirming or diverging** from institutional positioning — and does extreme retail crowding create a contrarian signal?
3. Is the dominant narrative **intact, evolving, or broken**, and how would a narrative shift affect the current multiple?
4. What does the institutional ownership trend reveal about **smart money conviction** in this name?

## Scoring

**SN Score (0-100)** = News sentiment (35%) + Social signals (15%) + Institutional positioning (35%) + Narrative alignment (15%)

News sentiment scoring: Predominantly positive, high volume, improving trajectory = 80-100; neutral or mixed = 40-60; negative, high-volume, deteriorating = 0-30.
Social signals scoring: Moderate positive interest, not at extreme crowding = 70-100; elevated but not extreme = 40-60; extreme crowding (caution signal) or highly negative = 0-30.
Institutional positioning scoring: Cluster buying by high-conviction funds, rising ownership concentration, analyst upgrades = 80-100; stable with mixed signals = 40-60; institutional selling while retail rises, analyst downgrades = 0-30.
Narrative alignment scoring: Dominant narrative intact and supported by recent events = 70-100; narrative in transition = 40-60; narrative broken or actively contested = 0-30.

## Red Flags

- Institutional ownership declining quarter-over-quarter while retail interest and social mentions spike
- High news volume driven by negative events (legal, regulatory, executive departures) rather than positive catalysts
- Social media sentiment at multi-year extremes (bullish crowding) — historically precedes mean reversion
- Narrative has shifted from growth story to turnaround or broken story without fundamental justification
- Analyst consensus rapidly downgrading price targets while management maintains guidance — credibility gap
- Sudden news sentiment shift (positive to negative) on high volume without a clear fundamental trigger
- Major institutional holders initiating sales while company is in a quiet period — insider information risk

## Output

- SN Score (0-100) with sub-factor breakdown
- News sentiment assessment: tone classification, volume trend, key themes, trajectory direction
- Social signal summary: mention volume vs. 30-day average, dominant sentiment, crowding risk level
- Narrative classification: current dominant story category, narrative health (intact/evolving/broken)
- Institutional positioning: top holder changes, hedge fund vs. long-only ratio shift, analyst rating trend
- Sentiment-to-fundamentals divergence flag: where sentiment materially leads or lags fundamental reality
- Actionable signal: sentiment tailwind / neutral / headwind with confidence level
