## Purpose

Determine whether the management team is competent, aligned with shareholders, and deploying capital wisely. Great businesses can be destroyed by poor management; mediocre businesses can be transformed by exceptional leadership. Management quality is often the swing factor in concentrated positions and turnaround situations.

The core question is: "Do the people running this business think and act like owners?"

## Key Dimensions

| Dimension | Sub-Skill | Core Question |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Leadership | leadership-assessment | Has this team built shareholder value before, and are they executing on their stated strategy? |
| Capital Allocation | capital-allocation | Are they deploying excess cash in the highest-return opportunities, or destroying value through indiscriminate M&A and buybacks? |
| Insider Activity | insider-activity | Are insiders putting their own money behind the story they're telling analysts? |
| Compensation Alignment | compensation-alignment | Is executive pay tied to shareholder outcomes, or are executives extracting value regardless of performance? |

## Synthesis

After running all sub-skills, answer these integrative questions:
1. Is this a **founder-led or professional management** situation — and does the incentive structure reflect that?
2. Is management **allocating capital at returns above the cost of capital** consistently over 5+ years?
3. Is there **skin in the game** — meaningful insider ownership, recent open-market purchases, and compensation tied to real performance metrics?
4. What is the **one specific governance failure** that would most quickly impair this investment?

## Scoring

**MG Score (0-100)** = Leadership (25%) + Capital allocation (30%) + Insider activity (20%) + Comp alignment (25%)

Capital allocation receives the highest weight because the impact of good or poor capital deployment compounds over time and is the most quantifiable dimension of management quality.

Sub-factor scoring guidance:
- Leadership (0-100): Weighted average of track record (40%), tenure stability (30%), and strategic consistency (30%)
- Capital allocation (0-100): EXCELLENT = 80-100; GOOD = 60-79; MIXED = 40-59; POOR = 0-39
- Insider activity (0-100): Net buying with cluster signals = 80-100; Neutral/diversification selling = 50-60; Net selling into weakness = 0-30
- Comp alignment (0-100): High equity, pay-for-performance correlation, clawback provisions = 80-100; High cash, comp rising while returns fall = 0-30

## Red Flags

- C-suite turnover exceeding one key executive per year over a 3-year period
- M&A acquisitions trading below the acquisition price after 2+ years
- Buybacks executed at peak prices followed by equity issuances at lower prices
- CEO total compensation in the top quartile of peers while total shareholder returns are in the bottom quartile
- Insider selling accelerating in clusters without pre-announced 10b5-1 plans
- Promises made at Investor Day not followed through within stated timelines
- Compensation structure with no clawback provisions and no minimum stock ownership requirements
- Founder/CEO with no meaningful independent board oversight (board rubber-stamping)

## Output

- MG Score (0-100) with sub-factor breakdown
- Leadership quality classification: STRONG / ADEQUATE / WEAK
- Capital allocation track record rating: EXCELLENT / GOOD / MIXED / POOR
- Insider activity signal: BULLISH / NEUTRAL / BEARISH
- Compensation alignment rating: ALIGNED / MIXED / MISALIGNED
- Top governance risk and monitoring trigger
- Recommended proxy vote considerations (if applicable)
