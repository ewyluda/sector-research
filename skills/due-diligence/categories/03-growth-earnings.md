## Purpose

Determine whether this company is genuinely growing and whether the earnings can be trusted. Growth is the most seductive metric in investing — but not all growth is equal. Revenue can be manufactured through channel stuffing, earnings can be inflated via one-time gains, management can lowball guidance to manufacture beats, and analyst estimates can converge around numbers that have no independent basis.

This category cuts through the noise to answer: Is growth real, durable, and accelerating — or is it illusory, concentrated, and running out of runway?

## Synthesis

After running all sub-skills, answer these integrative questions:
1. Is **revenue growth** driven by durable, diversified streams — or concentrated and at risk?
2. Is **earnings quality** high (OCF tracks net income) — or are accruals widening and non-recurring items recurring?
3. Is **management credible** — and do they tend to sandbag, hit, or miss their own guidance?
4. Is **analyst momentum** positive (revisions tracking up) or negative (quiet downward drift while the stock holds)?

## Scoring

**GE Score (0-100)** = Revenue growth (30%) + Earnings quality (30%) + Guidance credibility (20%) + Analyst momentum (20%)

- Revenue growth: composite of revenue growth rate vs. sector median and decomposition quality (concentration, acceleration); 80-100 = strong multi-stream growth above sector, 50-79 = solid single-driver growth, 0-49 = decelerating or concentrated
- Earnings quality: Earnings Quality Score (0-100) directly from earnings-quality sub-skill
- Guidance credibility: HIGH = 80-100, MODERATE = 50-79, LOW = 0-49 (converted from guidance-analysis credibility rating)
- Analyst momentum: net revision direction over 60 days and estimate dispersion; upgrades > downgrades and tightening dispersion = 70-100, flat = 40-60, downgrades > upgrades = 0-39

## Red Flags

- Revenue concentration >50% in a single product, geography, or customer segment
- Accrual ratio >15% (earnings materially outpacing cash generation)
- OCF consistently below net income for 3+ consecutive quarters
- Management classified as Optimist (consistently misses guidance) with no credible turnaround plan
- Analyst consensus revisions trending down while stock price remains flat (market complacency before re-rating)
- Non-recurring charges appearing in every annual period (structurally impaired business, not a one-time event)
- Guidance ranges so wide they always "hit" regardless of actual performance

## Output

- GE Score (0-100) with sub-factor breakdown
- Revenue decomposition table (stream, growth rate, % of revenue, margin contribution, acceleration status)
- Earnings Quality Score (0-100) with accrual ratio, OCF-to-NI ratio, non-recurring prevalence
- Guidance credibility rating: HIGH / MODERATE / LOW with supporting track record data
- Analyst estimate trend table (consensus EPS/revenue, revision direction, estimate dispersion)
- Key growth & earnings risks and monitoring triggers
